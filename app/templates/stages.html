报告里的「学习基础参考」是什么意思？

这一页解释模拟面试报告中「学习基础参考」那一部分：年级水平从哪里来，每一格代表什么，以及应该怎么看。

年级水平从哪里来

报告使用加州教育部发布的《学前／过渡幼儿园学习基础》（Preschool/Transitional Kindergarten Learning Foundations，简称 PTKLF）。它描述孩子在两个年龄段通常知道什么、能做什么：

{% for name, age in bands %}{{ name }}{{ age }}{% endfor %}
  • 学前（PTKLF Early）：约 3 岁到 4 岁半。
  • TK（PTKLF Later）：约 4 岁到 5 岁半。
  • K：PTKLF 只覆盖到 TK。报告里标为 K 的，是超出 PTKLF 范围、属于 K 阶段的能力，例如拼读单词和读句子。

应该怎么看

  • 涂色的格子表示「这次看到了这个水平的能力」。一次面试约 20 分钟，每个领域只有一到两道题，所以它说明的是当天看到的表现。
  • ★ 标出孩子所在的年级。涂色到 ★ 那一格，说明这个领域的表现和所在年级相当；超过 ★，说明已经表现出更高年级的能力。
  • 没有涂色不等于不会。可能是这次的题目没有考到，也可能是孩子当天紧张、没有说出来。报告里会写「本次未看到」。
  • 不同领域有快有慢是正常的。不需要每一行都在同一格。

What does the "Learning Foundations Reference" on the report mean?

This page explains the "Learning Foundations Reference" part of the mock interview report: where the grade levels come from, what each box stands for, and how to read it.

Where the grade levels come from

The report uses California's Preschool/Transitional Kindergarten Learning Foundations (PTKLF), published by the California Department of Education. They describe what children typically know and can do in two age ranges:

{% for name, age in bands %}{{ name }}{{ age }}{% endfor %}
  • Preschool (PTKLF Early): about 3 to 4½ years.
  • TK (PTKLF Later): about 4 to 5½ years.
  • Kindergarten: the PTKLF stop at TK. Skills marked Kindergarten on the report go beyond the PTKLF, such as decoding words and reading sentences.

How to read it

  • A coloured box means "skills at this level were seen today". One interview lasts about 20 minutes, with one or two tasks per area, so it describes what was seen that day.
  • The ★ marks your child's own class. Colour up to the ★ means the area matches their class; colour past the ★ means skills of a higher grade were shown.
  • An uncoloured box does not mean "cannot do". The tasks may not have covered it, or the child may have been nervous and not said it aloud. The report says "not yet shown today".
  • Different areas moving at different speeds is normal. The rows do not need to line up.
